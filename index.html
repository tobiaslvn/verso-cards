About this work

The same flower motif is painted by hand 52 times in a row, a meditation on repetition.

There are no two flowers that are the same, yet they are exactly the same. The balance between chaos and order expresses itself through everything in this way.

A handmade object carries the limitations of the person who made it, and in that it shares something grounded in what is real. A direct statement against the imitation of reality we are seeing today.
